At **WinAurex**, accessible via [winaurex.tech](https://winaurex.tech) and through our open-source tools (CLI, PowerShell scripts, and C# WPF GUI), we believe that **privacy is a fundamental right**.

Our primary mission is to **eliminate telemetry, tracking, and bloatware** from Windows operating systems. We practice the exact same philosophy with our own software and web services.

---

## 1. Zero Software Telemetry Guarantee

When you download, compile, or execute any WinAurex component on your machine:

- **No Personal Data Collected**: WinAurex does not collect names, email addresses, IP addresses, hardware identifiers, or usage statistics.
- **No Remote Phone-Home**: Neither the C# WPF GUI, the Python CLI engine, nor the PowerShell scripts transmit data back to our servers or any third-party infrastructure.
- **100% Local Execution**: All optimizations, registry tweaks, DISM package servicing, and image building are performed completely offline on your local computer.
- **Open Source Auditing**: All source code is publicly accessible on [GitHub](https://github.com/YTxFSGAMERz/WinAurex). Anyone can inspect and verify that no hidden tracking mechanisms exist.

---

## 2. Web Studio & Browser Storage

When using the [WinAurex Live Web Studio](/studio/):
- **Local Browser Memory**: All recipe configurations (preset selections, custom registry flags, autounattend XML generation) are processed entirely client-side using JavaScript in your browser.
- **No Form Data Transmission**: When you click "Download Recipe" or "Generate Unattended XML", the file is generated dynamically in your browser memory (Blob URL) without sending your choices to our backend.

## 3. Website Analytics & Advertising Cookies

When visiting [winaurex.tech](https://winaurex.tech), we utilize minimal third-party services to ensure site availability, speed, and sustainability:

### A. Vercel Analytics & Speed Insights
- We utilize **Vercel Analytics** and **Vercel Speed Insights** to monitor aggregated site performance (Core Web Vitals, page load latency, error rates).
- Vercel Analytics is privacy-friendly, does not use persistent cookies to track individuals across websites, and does not store personally identifiable information (PII).

### B. Google AdSense
- We display contextual advertisements served by **Google AdSense** (`ca-pub-6005307432943157`) to support hosting and continuous open-source maintenance.
- Google, as a third-party vendor, uses cookies (such as the DoubleClick cookie) to serve ads based on prior visits to this website or other websites on the internet.
- You may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting [Google Ads Settings](https://www.google.com/settings/ads) or through [aboutads.info](https://www.aboutads.info/choices/).
